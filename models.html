// The lineup

Which tool is better for what question?

Every model has a shape. Some reason hard, some write well, some know what happened this morning. Here is exactly what runs your council today, and a guide to the wider landscape.

// Running today

The council panel.

Council

Four models, four labs

Council is not another model. Each question goes to three models from different AI labs, each arguing one side, and a fourth from another lab judges. Every verdict ends with a receipt of exactly which models answered.

// One model or a panel

Council against one model.

Council is slower and costs more. If the question is easy, use one model.

One modelCouncil
AnswersOneThree, then one verdict
The case againstRarelyAlways, it is a seat
DisagreementHiddenShown under the verdict
ConfidenceSame tone for everythingHigh, medium or low
Which modelOne labThree labs on every question
Speed and costFast, cheapSlower, dearer

Use one model when

  • The question has one right answer.
  • You need a reply in two seconds.
  • You are drafting, coding or summarising something you already understand.

Use Council when

  • Being wrong would cost you money, time or a relationship.
  • Reasonable people would disagree.
  • You have already asked one AI and want to know how confident to be.

Strengths are drawn from public comparison charts, not our own testing. We do not build models. We call them through their providers’ APIs and put them into a structured argument. What we built is the argument.
