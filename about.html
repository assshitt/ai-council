// About

Council is a small project with one idea behind it: the questions that matter most are the ones with no single right answer, and those are exactly the questions a confident chatbot handles worst.

Where this came from

It started as an argument with a chatbot. Ask an AI whether to take a job, or whether an idea is worth building, and it answers in the same assured voice it uses for the boiling point of water. That voice is the problem. It hides the fact that reasonable people, and reasonable models, would disagree.

So we built it the other way round

Three models are given three different jobs and answer independently. A fourth reads all three and issues one verdict, with the disagreement left visible underneath. Where a claim can be checked against the outside world, it is.

// What we actually are

An orchestrator, not a lab

We do not train models. We rent frontier models through an API and put them into a structured argument.

Small and early

This is not a funded team. It is a working product with a free council you can use right now, and a roadmap we are honest about.

Honesty is the feature

Other tools compete on sounding capable. We would rather be the one that tells you when it is a coin flip, because that is the information you were missing.

Not the only ones thinking this

Multi-model panels are a live idea and others are building in this space. We think the gate, the roles and verification make ours different, but we did not invent the category.

// Where it is going

Shipped

The council

Fact gate, three roles, chairman verdict, external checking. Free, no login.

Next

Memory

Every debate and verdict saved, so Council can draw on what you have already decided.

The real goal

Outcomes

Asking, months later, how the decision actually went, and learning which reasoning led you right.

Models are frozen; they do not learn from you. But a system built around them can remember what you decided and what happened next, and that record gets more useful the longer you use it. That is the version of Council worth building.
