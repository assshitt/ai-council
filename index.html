// How it works

From question to verdict.

Four steps, in the same order, every time. The panel is picked for the kind of question you ask.

01

The gate

Council decides whether your question has one correct answer. Factual ones get a single line and an honest nudge, not a staged debate.

02

Three roles

Judgment questions convene a panel picked for the type of question: one argues for, one against, one works out how to make it better. They answer in parallel.

03

The chairman

A fourth model reads all three, picks the strongest reasoning and issues one verdict, with the disagreement left in view.

04

Verification

Planned for Plus: factual claims in the verdict get checked against an outside source. Not switched on in the free preview yet.

Drop only if your mocks were rising and you can name exactly what you will do differently. Otherwise take the seat and keep your options open.

// Group chat New

A group chat that reads before you sign.

Talk to the whole council at once. When the message moves money, three AIs from different labs agree on exactly what you meant and flag anything risky, so nothing is signed by mistake.

  • One room, three labsThe Advocate, the Critic and the Builder each come from a different AI lab. They read each other’s messages and push back by name.
  • Reads money the way you meant itType a transfer, swap or payment in plain words. The council agrees on the exact amount, asset, network and recipient.
  • Catches what one model missesA vague “half”, a name instead of an address, a missing time or slippage: the Critic flags it before anything is signed.
  • One final readingThe Chair returns the exact steps, a confidence level, and whether it is ready to sign or needs one more answer.

Council never touches your wallet or bank. It only reads the instruction.
