What every plan includes

No model paywalled

Plans differ by how many questions you ask, not by which models you are allowed to reach. Locking the good model behind the expensive tier is a pricing trick, not a product.

The fact gate

Ask something with one correct answer and Council answers in one line and tells you it is not what a panel is for. You are not billed for theatre.

Disagreement shown

Every verdict carries the split underneath it. Where the models disagreed is information you paid for, so you get to see it.

Your questions stay yours

We do not train models on your conversations. We could not if we wanted to — we do not own any.

Questions about cost

Why is this not free forever?

Every question runs three frontier models plus a chairman, so a single Council question costs several times what one chatbot reply costs. The free tier is real, but it has a floor.

Why would I pay when I already pay for ChatGPT?

Keep it. This is for the narrower set of questions where being wrong is expensive, and where one model cannot tell you how confident to be. Most people here use it for the decision that matters, not for everyday chat.

What happens if I hit the limit?

Nothing scary and no surprise bill. You are told you have reached the cap and can wait for the reset or upgrade. We do not silently keep charging.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, and access continues to the end of the period you already paid for.
